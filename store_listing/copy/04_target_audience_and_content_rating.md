# Google Play Console - App Content & Policy Declarations

Follow these settings for the "Policy and programs > App content" tasks in Google Play Console.

---

## 1. Privacy Policy
- Provide URL: https://github.com/zaryif/Minimal_os/blob/main/store_listing/copy/02_privacy_policy.md
  (Or host via GitHub Pages if preferred).

---

## 2. Ads Declaration
- Question: "Does your app contain ads?"
- Answer: "No, my app does not contain ads"

---

## 3. App Access
- Question: "Is all or part of your app restricted based on login credentials, memberships, location, or other forms of authentication?"
- Answer: "All functionality in my app is available without any restrictions"

---

## 4. Content Ratings (IARC)
Start questionnaire:
1. Category: Select "Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other"
2. Violence: No
3. Sexuality: No
4. Language: No
5. Controlled Substance: No
6. Miscellaneous:
   - Does the app natively allow users to interact or exchange content with other users? No
   - Does the app share the user's current and precise physical location with others? No
   - Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods? No
Result: Rating: Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0 (All ages).

---

## 5. Target Audience and Content
- Target age groups: Select "18 and over" (or "13-17" and "18 and over").
- Could your store listing appeal to children? Select "No".

---

## 6. Financial Features
- Question: "Does your app provide financial features?"
- Answer: "My app doesn't provide any financial features"

---

## 7. Government Apps
- Question: "Is your app developed by or on behalf of a government?"
- Answer: "No"
