## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Minimal OS is a refined, distraction-free Android launcher designed to eliminate digital clutter, reclaim attention, and turn your device into an intentional, productive workspace.

By replacing chaotic icon grids, notification badges, and algorithmic feeds with a clean typographic interface and a terminal command line, Minimal OS allows you to navigate your phone with clarity and purpose.

KEY FEATURES

1. FAST TYPOGRAPHIC HOME SCREEN
- Ultra-clean display featuring essential time, date, calendar agenda, and battery metrics.
- High-contrast AMOLED themes designed to reduce eye strain and conserve battery life.
- Zero icon noise: access apps through clean alphabetical text.

2. INTEGRATED TERMINAL COMMAND BAR
Control your device using an intuitive, natural command line:
- Tasks & Reminders: Prefix with "-" to capture tasks instantly (e.g., "- Review weekly report").
- Quick Notes: Prefix with "!" to save thoughts to your scratchpad without opening heavy apps.
- Calendar Events: Prefix with "*" to schedule events naturally (e.g., "* Team sync tomorrow at 3pm").
- Focus Timers: Prefix with "+" or "%" to launch Pomodoro and focus countdown sessions.
- System Actions: Set alarms with ":", trigger assistant searches with "?", or launch web queries seamlessly.
- Custom Aliases: Assign 1-character shortcuts (e.g., "z" for YouTube, "n" for Notes, "m" for Maps, "c" for Chrome, "calc" for Calculator).
- Interactive Command History Buffer: View past commands as numbered cards ([1], [2], [3]...); tap any card to re-execute or edit instantly.

3. UNIFIED WIDGETS DECK
- Swipe right from the home screen into a dedicated widgets page.
- At-a-glance access to active focus timers, pending tasks, recent notes, battery status, and upcoming calendar appointments.
- Add standard Android system widgets in a clean, contained view without cluttering your main screen.

4. MINIMAL MEMO & SCRATCHPAD
- Fast slide-up memo sheet accessible from anywhere on the home screen.
- Manage focused task checklists and quick markdown-compatible scratchpad notes.
- Integrated quick timer pill to launch study and work sprints instantly.

5. UNIFIED NOTIFICATION HUB
- Swipe left into a consolidated, categorized notification center.
- Filter incoming alerts by Messages, Calls, Email, and Apps without getting sucked into endless notification shade loops.
- Silence notification dots and badge anxiety on your main home screen.

6. PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE
- 100% offline: No telemetry, no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no ads.
- All notes, tasks, and command history remain securely stored on your local device.
- No network permissions required for core launcher operation.

7. DEEP CUSTOMIZATION
- Themes: Pure AMOLED Black, Space Gray, and Nordic Snow palettes.
- Adaptive luminance engine ensuring crisp WCAG AAA legibility on every accent color.
- Fine-grained typography, widget ordering, and gesture sensitivity controls.
- Pinned favorite apps for immediate one-tap launching.

Transform your relationship with your smartphone. Simplify your screen, master your time, and experience the calmness of Minimal OS.
